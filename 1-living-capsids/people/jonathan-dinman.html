Jonathan D. Dinman, PhD

Professor, Cell Biology and Molecular Genetics

Director, Institute for Bioscience and Biotechnology Research (since 2024)

About

Jonathan Dinman directs IBBR and is a professor in Cell Biology and Molecular Genetics. He previously chaired the department and directed the MOCB graduate program, and has taught virology, RNA biology, and an honors course on globalization, climate change and infectious disease.

Research

The lab studies how cells control gene expression at the level of protein synthesis. Viral programmed −1 ribosomal frameshift signals serve as a probe for how ribosomes keep reading frame, and the group has extended this to frameshifting in alphaviruses, cellular frameshift signals, ribosome structure and function, and the integrated stress response.
