Tijana Ivanovic, PhD

Chief, Single Virion Biology and Biophysics Unit, NIAID

NIH Distinguished Scholar

About

Tijana Ivanovic developed TIRF-based single-particle methods for studying viral membrane fusion as a postdoc with Steve Harrison at Harvard, then ran her own lab at Brandeis University before moving to NIH in 2023.

Research

The unit studies how viruses enter cells and assemble, and how particle structure affects infection and adaptation. The lab showed that the shape of pleomorphic virions such as influenza filaments can help them resist antibody and drug blockade of membrane fusion, and uses single-particle imaging to study reovirus assembly and transcription.
