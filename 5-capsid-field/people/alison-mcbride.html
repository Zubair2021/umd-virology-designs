Alison A. McBride, PhD

Chief, DNA Tumor Virus Section, NIAID

About

Alison McBride began studying papillomaviruses as a postdoctoral fellow at the National Cancer Institute and joined NIAID in 1994. She is a Fellow of the American Academy of Microbiology and a section editor of PLOS Pathogens.

Research

The McBride lab studies how papillomaviruses replicate and persist in epithelial cells. HPV causes about 5% of human cancers. The viral E2 protein tethers the genome to host mitotic chromosomes so it is passed to daughter cells, and viral replication starts at host chromatin under replication stress. The lab works mainly in primary human keratinocytes.

  • Papillomavirus
  • HPV persistence
  • Genome partitioning
  • DNA damage response
  • Keratinocytes
