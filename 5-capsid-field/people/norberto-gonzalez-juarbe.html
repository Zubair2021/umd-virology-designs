Norberto Gonzalez-Juarbe, PhD

Associate Professor, Cell Biology and Molecular Genetics

About

Norberto Gonzalez-Juarbe studies how microbes cause disease and how host cell death and the microbiome shape infection and immunity.

Research

His lab studies how respiratory viruses, including influenza and SARS-CoV-2, damage the heart through inflammation, metabolic change and cell death. The group also investigates Streptococcus pneumoniae in the nasopharynx and lungs, bacteriophage endolysins as anti-biofilm treatments, protein vaccine candidates, and engineered phages and commensal bacteria against antimicrobial-resistant pathogens.
