Kim Y. Green, PhD

Chief, Caliciviruses Section, NIAID

About

Kim Green has spent her career on viruses that cause gastroenteritis, joining NIAID's Laboratory of Infectious Diseases in 1986. She serves on the ICTV Caliciviridae Study Group.

Research

Noroviruses are a leading cause of acute gastroenteritis, and no approved vaccines or antivirals exist. The Green lab builds experimental systems for norovirus replication and immunity, compares diverse caliciviruses to find shared features, and uses decades of archived specimens to trace how the dominant GII.4 strain has evolved, to guide vaccine design.
