Andrew J. Broadbent, VetMB, PhD

Assistant Professor, Animal and Avian Sciences

About

Andrew Broadbent trained in veterinary medicine at Cambridge and virology at Imperial College London, did postdoctoral work at NIH, and led a research group at The Pirbright Institute before joining UMD in 2021.

Research

The Broadbent lab studies the molecular basis of avian virus replication and pathogenesis, to improve control of viral diseases in poultry and protect public health. Current work centers on segmented RNA viruses: infectious bursal disease virus, avian reovirus and avian influenza virus.
