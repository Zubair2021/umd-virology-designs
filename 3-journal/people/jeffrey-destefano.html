About

Jeffrey DeStefano is a professor in Cell Biology and Molecular Genetics and co-directs the Virology Program. He teaches General Virology (BSCI437) and the program's graduate Molecular Virology course (CBMG688K).

Research

The DeStefano lab studies HIV and picornavirus replication and recombination, focusing on HIV reverse transcriptase and the poliovirus RNA-dependent RNA polymerase. The group develops nucleic acid and xeno-nucleic acid (XNA) aptamers against viral targets such as HIV-1 integrase and the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein, and uses next-generation sequencing to study HIV genetic diversity and reverse transcriptase fidelity.
