About

George Belov studies the molecular biology of small positive-strand RNA viruses that depend heavily on their host cells.

Research

Positive-strand RNA viruses include poliovirus, hepatitis C, dengue and Zika, and many lack vaccines or drugs. The Belov lab studies how these viruses rewire membrane metabolism to build replication organelles. Because distant viruses share host factors, the work points toward broad-spectrum antivirals, and the lab is also developing mucosal vectored vaccines against enterovirus D68.
