Lai-Xi Wang, PhD

Distinguished University Professor, Chemistry and Biochemistry

About

Lai-Xi Wang works at the intersection of bioorganic chemistry, glycobiology and immunology. He previously held faculty posts at the University of Maryland School of Medicine and the Shanghai Institute of Organic Chemistry.

Research

The Wang lab studies the structures and functions of glycans and glycoproteins. Combining synthetic chemistry, molecular and structural biology and immunology, the group examines how glycans shape host-pathogen recognition, and develops chemoenzymatic synthesis, site-specific antibody glycoengineering, and carbohydrate-based HIV vaccines aimed at the glycan epitopes of broadly neutralizing antibodies against gp120.
