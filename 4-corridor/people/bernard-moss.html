Bernard Moss, MD, PhD

NIH Distinguished Investigator, Laboratory of Viral Diseases, NIAID

About

Bernard Moss is known for foundational work on mRNA cap structures, poxvirus replication, viral immune-defense molecules and viral vectors. He is a member of the National Academy of Sciences and a past president of the American Society for Virology.

Research

The Moss group studies how viruses enter cells, express their genomes, assemble and evade host immunity, using poxviruses as a central model. This work supports antiviral drug design and live and subunit recombinant vaccines, including recent mpox vaccine candidates, and the use of engineered viruses as expression vectors.
