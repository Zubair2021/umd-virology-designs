Patrick T. Dolan, PhD

Stadtman Investigator; Chief, Quantitative Virology and Evolution Unit, NIAID

About

Patrick Dolan is an experimental virologist and computational biologist. He has led NIAID's Quantitative Virology and Evolution Unit since 2021.

Research

The lab studies how positive-sense RNA viruses evolve and infect hosts, focusing on variable, heterogeneous processes during infection. It combines high-resolution sequencing, live-cell microscopy and phylogenetics, with projects on within-host evolution, deep mutational scanning of enteroviruses, and evolutionary biochemistry of emerging RNA viruses.

  • Deep mutational scanning
  • Enteroviruses
  • Single-cell sequencing
