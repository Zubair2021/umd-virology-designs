Format

How the meetings work

Since September 2001, faculty, trainees and postdocs have met monthly on the University of Maryland campus for one hour. A typical meeting draws 30 to 45 participants.

Each month, two faculty laboratories present their current research. Presenters include faculty, postdocs and senior trainees, and the talks are followed by open discussion.

Afterward, some laboratories meet in smaller groups, and trainees use the time to meet with dissertation committee members and co-mentors.

Attendance is required for all pre-doctoral trainees.
