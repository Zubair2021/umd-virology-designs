Inter-Institution Virology Program

Graduate training in virology across four institutions.

Specialized graduate training in host-virus interactions and in viral immunology and vaccine design, with faculty mentors at the University of Maryland, the National Institutes of Health, the Virginia-Maryland College of Veterinary Medicine and the Institute for Bioscience and Biotechnology Research.

Specialized training

Two research tracks

The program concentrates its training on two central areas of modern virology.

01

Host-virus interactions

How viruses hijack host cells, how cells fight back with innate immunity, and how viruses evade those defenses.

Read more → 02

Viral immunology & vaccine design

Engineered antigens for broad antibody and T cell responses, and mucosal vaccine platforms against HIV, influenza, RSV, SARS-CoV-2 and herpesviruses.

Program highlights

What the program offers

Leading research laboratories

Train in laboratories at UMD, NIH, Vet Med and IBBR, spanning academic and government science.

Dedicated mentoring

Each trainee works with mentors who advise on research and career development.

An annual retreat

Faculty, postdocs and trainees from all program laboratories meet for a day of talks, posters and discussion.

Didactic training

Graduate courses in molecular virology, viral pathogenesis, immunology and viral vectors.
